Motion Guide

Timing

Term / Meaning / Example
Duration
How long one transition takes.
0.6s = 600ms
Delay
How long an animation waits before starting.
A 0.2s delay starts after 200ms.
Stagger
The extra start delay between items.
0.2s stagger: 0s, 0.2s, 0.4s…
Hold
How long content stays before changing again.
Typewriter holds a word for 1.8s.
Loop
The time for one complete repeat.
Auto Scroll Feed repeats every 70s.
Scroll distance
How far you scroll to complete an effect.
120svh spans 1.2 small viewport heights.
